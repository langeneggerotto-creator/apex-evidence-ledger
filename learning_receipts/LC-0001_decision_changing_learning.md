# Learning Receipt LC-0001

Status: DESIGNED_NOT_PROVEN / DECISION_CHANGE_RECORDED

## Source
APEX discovery work on Inferensys, multi-agent orchestration, digital twins, gap governance, and receipt architecture.

## Observation
More agents, models, twins, and modules do not automatically create better outcomes. Better outcomes require orchestration, evidence, governance, calibration, and decision traceability.

## Principle extracted
Evidence becomes learning only when it changes future decisions.

## Decision changed
Before: APEX Learning Center could have become a content library or summary archive.

After: APEX Learning Center is now defined as a decision-improvement engine that stores learning receipts only when learning changes future decisions.

## Evidence
Conversation-derived architecture changed from content storage toward learning receipts and decision-changing learning.

## Gap reduced
Learning Measurement Gap.

## Reusability
High. Applies to Daily Gap Governor, Evidence Ledger, Learning Center, Digital Twins, Perspective Swarm Engine, OCODE, and APEX Mirror.

## Truth boundary
This receipt records a design decision change. It does not prove that automated Learning Center ingestion works yet.
